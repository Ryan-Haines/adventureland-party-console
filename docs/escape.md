# Party escape

Escape controls the living fighters of the party; the merchant and saved event preferences are unchanged. The dashboard polls `/party-api/escape`. POST to that endpoint starts an operation; POST `/party-api/escape/resume` releases it. A new Town, travel, or monster-navigation command also releases it. The survival owner decides the escape; movement makes every Blink, Magiport and walk.

A member that is already dead when the escape starts leaves it at once to its own death recovery, which respawns it and walks it to the same home. When a mage is alive, the first living mage leads: it Blinks to an outdoor Town spawn or a map door/transporter landing, ranking visible threat clearance before proximity, then Magiports each other participant in turn (the warrior, the priest, then the rest by name) while its MP covers Magiport. A member its MP cannot cover walks the safe path. With no mage, every participant walks. Door landings are offset to avoid automatically crossing a portal. Walkability is checked, but unseen monsters can still make a landing dangerous. Invitations require the current operation's mage, recipient, and stage.

Each Blink or Magiport stage ends on its reply: accepted moves to the next stage, refused sends that member walking, and a reply that never comes counts as lost after the skill's cooldown and the largest recent round trip, so no stage has a deadline. A participant that dies or stops reporting during the escape leaves the remaining stages to its own recovery or to the walk; the escape goes on for the rest, and every remaining participant walks when that member is the mage. A walk stage climbs the movement owner's walk ladder while it makes progress.

Every escape spend is a push into the turns executor, so it asks the call budget and shares the potion cooldown with the other potion spends. Each fighter drinks HP first below its "HP potion below" setting; then MP up to its escape budget: the warrior Hardshell plus two Dashes, the priest Curse plus Party Heal plus Heal, and other classes full MP, after MP reduction and capped at maximum MP; non-mages then drink HP up to full. The priest's escape heal is the survival owner's one heal choice, and its Curse goes only on an existing party attacker after a heal that did not land. The warrior uses Hardshell, Charge once per shield cycle, and collision-checked retreat Dashes. A character holding a jacko Scares the monsters that target it before it Blinks, is ported or walks.

The party regroups at the respawn point of the map of the death (the movement owner's death goal). A blocked recovery stays visible and is not reported complete. Restarting the coordinator during rescue enters recovery.

Success and recovered failure both retain a movement/combat hold until Resume automation or explicit movement. Normal regeneration and priest healing continue while holding. The operation ID invalidates stale local casts and invitations; recovery movement uses cancellation guards so late cleanup cannot stop a replacement route.

Tests use simulated snapshots and skill calls; validation must not trigger a live escape, change event attendance, or intentionally kill characters.
